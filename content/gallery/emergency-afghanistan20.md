EMERGENCY is a charitable NGO that provides free medical treatment to victims of war. To coincide with the 20–year anniversary of the U.S. invasion of Afghanistan, Accurat brought to life Afghanistan20: a data–driven historical document showing the aftereffects of 9/11 on the Afghani people and EMERGENCY’s charitable efforts in the region featuring some visualizations made with RAWGraphs
