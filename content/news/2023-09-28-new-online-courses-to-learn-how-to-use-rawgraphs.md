We are happy to announce that we have just launched a series of live online courses to help you discovering RAWGraphs. You can find the calendar of upcoming activities [here.](https://www.rawgraphs.io/courses)

### What will you learn?

During the 2.5 webinar you will learn:

- How to insert your data in RAWGraphs and some tips on how to prepare your data
- How to dynamically load and update your data from Google Spreadsheet
- How to create a visualization and how to use it to make sense of your data
- How to refine the visualization in RAWGraphs and export it
- How to export projects
- Some information about the project and the next steps for the RAWGraphs team

You will also receive the slides of the webinar, project, and data sample to practice and all the didactical material used during the webinar.

### Why should you participate?

First of all, because is a great way to improve your skills and learn a free software that is used by thousands of users worldwide. Second of all, because you will directly support a free and open-source tool. The more financial support we receive from sponsors and users, the more time we will be able to dedicate to the project.

### Who is the teacher?

The teacher of the webinar is Giorgio Uboldi, co-founder of [Calibro](https://calib.ro/) and previously a member of [DensityDesign](https://densitydesign.org/) that, together with [Inmagik](https://inmagik.com/en), are the three partners involved in the creation and maintenance of RAWGraphs. He's been part of the RAWGraphs team from the beginning and he's a designer focused on data-driven projects and research.

If you have more questions please contact us at [hello@rawgraphs.io.](http://hello@rawgraphs.io)
