A member of our development team recently participated in the 28th episode of the ["On Data And Design" podcast](http://www.ondataanddesign.com/), hosted by Darjan Hil. The episode focused on tools for data visualization.

The podcast featured guests from various data visualization platforms:

- Neil Richards presented Tableau, a widely used software for creating statistical data visualizations in corporate settings.
- Stijn Simoens introduced Datylon, a software that integrates with Adobe Illustrator to facilitate the creation of visualizations using templates.
- Tommaso Elli from RAWGraphs

During the podcast, we provided a demonstration of RAWGraphs. The demo utilized data from the [Bechdel test movie list](https://bechdeltest.com/), showcasing the tool's capabilities in quickly handling and visualizing a tabular dataset. The presentation also included an introduction to a new RAWGraphs feature: the ability to add custom chart templates. This functionality significantly expands the tool's expressive possibilities, allowing users to create and implement visualization types tailored to their specific needs.
