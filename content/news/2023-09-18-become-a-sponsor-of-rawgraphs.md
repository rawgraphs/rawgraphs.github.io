We have some exciting news to share! RAWGraphs has just launched a fundraising campaign through GitHub Sponsor, and we're inviting you to be part of our journey.

For those who might not be familiar with us, RAWGraphs is an open-source project developed by the [DensityDesign Research Lab at the Politecnico di Milano](https://densitydesign.org/), [Calibro](https://calib.ro/) and [Inmagik](https://inmagik.com/). . Our mission is simple: to create a easy-to-use and accessible software to visualize data.

To pursue our mission and be able to dedicate more time to the project, after a [succesfull crowdfuding campaing](/news/2019-12-15-crowdfunding-campaign-thank-you) we launched 4 years ago, we decided to launch a new Fundraising Campaign on GitHub Sponsor.

You are free to make a one-time donation or support us with a recurring monthly donation. Every penny counts.

### Why should you consider sponsoring RAWGraphs on GitHub?

**Empower Creativity**: Your support helps us continue our mission of making data visualization accessible to all, regardless of their technical background .

**Sustain Quality**: Open-source projects require resources to maintain and improve. By sponsoring RAWGraphs, you ensure that we can keep providing high-quality visualizations and stay up-to-date.

**Community-Driven**: RAWGraphs thrives on community involvement. As a sponsor, you become part of our community and help shape our future.

**Boost Data Literacy:** Your contribution aids in our broader goal of enhancing data literacy in all the contexts, especially in teaching and research.

Join us in this  journey by visiting our [**GitHub sponsor page**](https://github.com/rawgraphs) to learn more about sponsorship levels and perks. Whether you're an individual who's benefited from RAWGraphs or an organization that values open-source tools, your support matters.

Together, we can ensure that RAWGraphs continues to flourish, innovate, and empower individuals and organizations to visualize data creatively and effectively.
