[FOSDEM](https://fosdem.org/) is a two-day event organised by volunteers to promote the widespread use of free and open source software. The event  is a free and it's a unique place for software developers and researchers to meet, share ideas and collaborate.

Usually taking place in the beautiful city of Brussels (Belgium), FOSDEM is widely recognised as the best such conference in Europe. This year the event took place online and we were very lucky to be able to attend and present our project and some updates.

You can find the [talk](https://archive.fosdem.org/2021/schedule/event/open_research_rawgraphs/) here. We hope to participate to FOSDEM in the future. A special thanks goes to the organisers and the many volunteers who help out during the weekend.
