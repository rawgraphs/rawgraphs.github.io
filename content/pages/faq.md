faqs:
  - question: Why can't I change font size and font family in RAWGraphs?
    answer: RAWGraphs has always been conceived as tool to produce semifinished visualizations. That's why we decided to not focus on adding these kind of functionalities but rather providing tidy and open vector files that can be easily edited in any vector graphics editor.
  - question: Do I have to cite RAWGraphs when I use it?
    answer: No. You are free to use RAWGraphs for any kind of work and you don't have to mention RAWGraphs. Anyway we are always curios to see it's always appreciated. Feel free to submit your work for our [gallery](/gallery) or mention us on [Twitter](https://twitter.com/rawgraphs). If you have found RAWGraphs useful in your research, or if you want to reference it in your work, please consider to cite the [paper](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3125571.3125585) we presented at CHItaly 2017.
  - question: Can I use and modify the code of RAWGraphs?
    answer: 'Of course! RAWGraphs is free and open source software, if you are a developer (or you know some) have a look at our [GitHub repositories](https://github.com/rawgraphs). Possibilities are endless, they include: running a headless RAWGraphs, making a new UI, adding and modifying charts.'
  - question: Why is RAWGraphs free and open source?
    answer: |-
      It’s a recurrent discussion among us. Here is a list of reasons why RAWGraphs will always be free (and open) and why we decided to launch a crowdfunding campaign:

      1.  As designers and/or developers dealing with data visualization, **we acknowledge that our work would certainly be much different if many free and open source solutions didn't exist.** We think about many existing projects like [Gephi](https://gephi.org/), [Open Refine](http://openrefine.org/), [D3js](https://d3js.org/) or [Scriptographer](https://scriptographer.org/) (R.I.P), which we use for free and that shape our processes and our skills. **We wanted to pay back our contribution** making people able to play with visualization and develop their data-literacy for free, or at least without the need to pay on a regular basis.
      2.  The RAWGraphs team members work full time as researchers, professors, designers and developers. Some are involved in academic work, while others are successfully managing independent design and development studios. **RAWGraphs has always been a side project and we are happy to keep as it is. We use it in our projects and during teaching activities and we are not interested in transform RAWGraphs into our core project.** Still, **we need the resources to seriously dedicate time to it** and to improve what we have. We think donations are the best way to achieve this goal.
      3.  Transforming RAWGraphs in a paid software would mean scaling up the project and dedicate a lot of time (and money) on other activities, such as managing users/clients, payments, client support, business development, etc. **Since we would like to keep it as minimal as possible, we prefer to use all the resources working on core features, rather than defining strategies to make money out of it.** We are probably better designers than entrepreneurs.
      4.  **RAWGraphs is mainly meant for producing raw visualisations**, that still needs some graphical attuning. There are already many applications allowing you to create well designed viz, ready for publication. These softwares (such as [Datawrapper](https://www.datawrapper.de/), [Flourish](https://flourish.studio/), the all-in-one [Tableau](https://www.tableau.com/) and many others) are doing great. We love and use them, and we are not interested in making a clone of them.
      5.  Our users like RAWGraphs ALSO because is free. **Being free and open, makes it easier for students, teachers and researchers to use them in courses, MOOC and workshops.** We thought that an occasional payment would suit better this kind of usage rather than licenses or subscriptions.
      6.  RAWGraphs was born inside a public university ([Politecnico di Milano](http://www.design.polimi.it/en/)) and **we like the idea that something born in a public institution stays free for everyone.**
  - question: Can I work with confidential data? Do you upload your data to a server?
    answer: Even though RAWGraphs is a web app, the data you insert will be processed only by the web browser. No server-side operations or storages are performed, no one will see, touch or copy your data.
